# Privacy Policy (自然音に包まれながらナンプレ)


**Last updated: October 20, 2026**  
**Operator: Trierr** (Japanese reading: トリエラ / Toriera)  
The App is operated by an individual. Trierr is the operator's trade name / brand name.

This policy explains how information is handled in the Sudoku puzzle app “自然音に包まれながらナンプレ” (short name and device display name: “自然音ナンプレ”, the “App”), distributed through Google Play and the Amazon Appstore.

## 1. About the App

The App is a 9×9 Sudoku puzzle game intended for people aged 16 and older. You can play while listening to ambient sounds such as a campfire or a stream. Distribution is planned in Japan, English-speaking markets, and the EU.

The App has no account registration, in-app purchases, or cloud storage operated by the operator. It has no feature that sends game scores or play history to the operator's servers. Random puzzle generation and hint calculations run on your device without sending the board to an external API. Ambient audio is bundled with the App; it is not streamed, and the App does not record through your microphone. OS backups are described in Section 2. Advertising services collect and transmit information separately, as explained in Section 3.

## 2. Information stored on your device

The App stores the following information in its local app storage to provide game features. The operator has no feature for remotely retrieving or viewing these saved records.

| Information | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Current puzzle, difficulty, entered digits, candidate notes, undo history, elapsed time, and completion status | Resuming games and maintaining progress |
| Hint content and usage count, rewarded-ad viewing and reward-processing status | Providing hints and preventing duplicate reward processing |
| Clear counts and best times by difficulty | Displaying records |
| Puzzle history, identifiers for completed plays, and prepared puzzles | Reducing repeated puzzles, preventing duplicate completion records, and preparing puzzles |
| Display language, selected ambient audio, and ambient volume | Remembering preferences |
| Clear counter for ad frequency, identifier of a play eligible for an ad, and last full-screen ad display time | Controlling how often ads appear |
| Diagnostic records of save, hint, and advertising results or errors (up to the latest 100 entries) | Checking App behavior and investigating problems |

The App has no feature that automatically sends these game records or its own diagnostic records to the operator's servers.

The App does not explicitly disable Android automatic backup. Depending on your device, OS, and settings, this information may be included in OS or device-provider backup and device-transfer services and may be restored. Consult the relevant backup service's settings and policies for its handling and deletion controls.

## 3. Advertising and third-party data processing

### 3.1 Google AdMob

The Google Play and Amazon releases use Google LLC's Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to display banner ads, rewarded ads for hints, and interstitial (full-screen) ads after puzzle completion.

For advertising, measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention, the advertising SDK may collect information and share it with Google and advertising partners. This includes IP addresses (which may indicate approximate location), advertising and other identifiers, app/ad interactions, and diagnostics. This is separate from the local game records in Section 2; the App does not send those records to the operator.

For retention, processing locations, recipients, and controls for Google's data processing, see:

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure](https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure)

### 3.2 Advertising consent and choices

In applicable regions, including the EEA (EU member states and other participating countries), the UK, and Switzerland, the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to present required consent and privacy choices according to the user's region and Google's message configuration. Processing that requires consent will follow those choices. Personalized advertising will depend on applicable requirements and the user's choices.

Where required, the App's settings provide an entry point to advertising privacy options so users can change or withdraw consent. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal. Declining personalized advertising does not necessarily stop all communication or processing needed for ad delivery and fraud prevention.

Android settings may allow you to delete or reset your advertising ID. Available controls vary by device and OS.

## 4. Device access and analytics

The App uses an internet connection for features such as loading ads. Its game features do not request access to contacts, photos, documents, the camera, the microphone, or precise GPS location.

The current game code does not include a separate analytics or crash reporting SDK such as Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics. Advertising SDK interaction and diagnostic data are described in Section 3.

## 5. Information received through support requests

If you contact the operator by email, the operator receives your email address and message and uses them to respond, investigate, and resolve your request. This information is retained for as long as necessary to handle the request and deleted when no longer needed.

## 6. Retention and deletion

You can delete local game records and settings by clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App. The record-deletion feature in the App's settings deletes clear counts and best times by difficulty. It does not delete all saved information, such as the current board, puzzle history, and preferences. Clearing local data or uninstalling the App may not delete copies held by backup services. The operator cannot remotely delete records on your device.

Uninstalling the App does not itself delete information already received by advertising services. For retention and deletion of data handled by Google, consult Google's policies and settings.

## 7. International processing and your rights

Advertising services may process information outside your country or region. Google's Privacy Policy explains its international transfer mechanisms and safeguards.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict processing of, object to processing of, or obtain a portable copy of personal data, and to withdraw consent. Contact the operator using Section 10 about information handled by the operator. For information handled by Google, you can also use the contacts and controls described in Google's policies. You may lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority.

## 8. Age group

The App is intended for people aged 16 and older and is not offered as a service directed to children under 16. The operator does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. Please contact the operator using Section 10 if you have concerns.

## 9. Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated when App features or service providers change. Changes and the updated date will be posted on this page. Important changes will also be communicated in the App where necessary.
